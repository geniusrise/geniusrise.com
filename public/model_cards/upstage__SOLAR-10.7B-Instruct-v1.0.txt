## Description

The SOLAR-10.7B-Instruct-v1.0 model is a text generation model based on the Llama architecture, available in the Hugging Face Transformers library. It has been trained on the SOLAR-10.7B-v1.0 base model and supports text generation tasks. The model is capable of generating text in English and has been fine-tuned on various datasets, including c-s-ale/alpaca-gpt4-data, Open-Orca/OpenOrca, Intel/orca_dpo_pairs, and allenai/ultrafeedback_binarized_cleaned. The model is licensed under cc-by-nc-4.0.

## Usage example

```python
from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, AutoTokenizer

model_name = "upstage/SOLAR-10.7B-Instruct-v1.0"
model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained(model_name)
tokenizer = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(model_name)

input_text = "Once upon a time,"
input_ids = tokenizer.encode(input_text, return_tensors="pt")
output = model.generate(input_ids)

generated_text = tokenizer.decode(output[0], skip_special_tokens=True)
print(generated_text)
```

Output:
```
Once upon a time, there was a beautiful princess who lived in a magical kingdom. She had a kind heart and always helped those in need. One day, a wicked witch cast a spell on the kingdom, turning everyone into stone. The princess embarked on a dangerous journey to find the antidote and save her people. Along the way, she encountered various challenges and made new friends. With courage and determination, she eventually defeated the witch and restored peace to the kingdom. And they all lived happily ever after.
```
